Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

The short version. Commandeck has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you. Everything you create stays on your device.

Who we are

Commandeck is an Android application developed by David Ferrandiz, an independent developer. In this policy, “the app” means the Commandeck Android application, and “we” means the developer.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for a name, an email address, a phone number or any other identifier. There is no registration and no user account. The app requests a single Android permission, INTERNET, which it uses to download public Magic: The Gathering card data. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other device data.

The app contains no advertising software, no analytics software and no crash reporting software. No third-party tracking libraries are included in the build.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved in the app’s private storage on your phone, where only the app can read it:

  • The decks you create, including their names, cards and commanders
  • Your recent card searches
  • A local cache of card data and images, so the app works without a connection and uses less data
  • Any deck analysis generated by the on-device AI feature

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings.

Services the app connects to

To show card information the app requests public data from the following services. Like any request over the internet, these receive your device’s IP address and a standard request header. We do not send them anything about you, and we do not receive anything back about you.

  • Scryfall — card data, prices and card images. See the Scryfall privacy policy.
  • playgroup.gg — aggregated Commander play statistics.
  • GitHub Pages — the app downloads pre-computed data files published by this project. See the GitHub privacy statement.

On-device AI

Some deck analysis is generated using Google’s on-device Gemini Nano model through ML Kit, on devices that support it. This processing happens entirely on your phone; your decks are not sent to Google or to us. Google Play services may download the model itself to your device. On devices without support, the feature is simply unavailable and the app falls back to its standard analysis.

Links to card shops

Card pages include a link to buy the card from an online retailer, chosen based on your device’s region setting. Following one of these links opens your browser and takes you to that retailer’s website, where their own privacy policy applies. These links may include an affiliate code, which means the retailer may pay us a commission if you make a purchase. This does not change the price you pay, and it does not identify you to us — we receive only anonymous aggregate totals from the retailer.

The region is determined locally on your device from your Android locale setting. It is never sent anywhere.

Children

Commandeck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on your behalf. All data the app creates lives on your device and is under your control: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it permanently.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play.
