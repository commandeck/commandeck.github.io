Build better Commander decks.

Commandeck is a free Android app for Magic: The Gathering Commander players. Search cards, build decks, and see how your list compares to what the rest of the format is actually playing.

  • Deck builderBuild and edit 100-card decks with live format checks, colour identity validation and mana curve breakdowns.
  • Commander insightsFor each commander, the cards other players run, grouped by theme, with real inclusion rates and sample sizes.
  • Deck analysisCompare your curve against the average deck for your commander, and see what you are missing and what you are overloading.
  • Opening hand simulatorDraw test hands with London mulligan rules and exact hypergeometric odds for keeping, flooding and screwing.
  • Card searchThe full Scryfall catalogue, with new sets and recently printed cards surfaced as they release.
  • Works offlineYour decks live on your device. No account, no sign-up, no ads.
