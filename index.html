Build better Commander decks.

A free Android app for Magic: The Gathering Commander players. Search cards, build decks, and see how your list measures up against what the rest of the format is actually playing.

  • Deck builderBuild and edit 100-card decks with live singleton checks, colour identity validation and mana curve breakdowns.
  • Commander insightsFor any commander, the cards other players actually run — grouped by theme, with real inclusion rates and sample sizes.
  • Deck analysisCompare your curve against the average deck for your commander, and see what you are missing and where you are overloaded.
  • Opening handDraw test hands under London mulligan rules, with exact hypergeometric odds for keeping, flooding and screwing.
  • Card searchThe full Scryfall catalogue, with new sets and recently printed cards surfaced as they release.
  • Yours, on your deviceYour decks live on your phone and work offline. No account, no sign-up, no ads.

Availability

Commandeck is in development and will be released on Google Play. This page will carry the download link when it goes live.

Questions or bug reports

Write to ferry.applications@gmail.com. Reports of a card that renders wrong, a rule the builder gets wrong, or a commander with bad data are all welcome.

Who makes this

Commandeck is built by one person. I am an Android developer, I have been playing Commander for years, and I got tired of brewing with a browser on one screen, a spreadsheet on the other and three tabs of inclusion rates in between. So I built the thing I kept wishing existed.

All of it is mine to keep running: the deck builder, the crawler that gathers play data every night, the hypergeometric maths behind the opening hands. There is no company behind this, no investors, and no plan to ever put a feature behind a payment.

Supporting the project

If Commandeck saved you time and you feel like helping, you can buy me a coffee. It buys me evenings, which is the only thing this project actually runs on.

To be clear about what it does not buy: nothing in the app is locked behind it, no feature is reserved for supporters, and nothing ever will be. Commandeck is free because it should be.
